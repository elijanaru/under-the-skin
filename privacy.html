Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026
In short
  • You can use Underskin without an account. Then everything stays only on your phone.
  • If you create an account, a copy of your data is kept on our server in the EU so you can sign in on another phone. Only you can read it.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and never use tracking or analytics.
  • You can delete everything at any time inside the app.

Who we are

Underskin is a cycle-tracking and wellbeing app. For any privacy question, write to elumiebusiness@gmail.com.

What the app stores

What you enter: your name, birthday (optional), country, profile photo (optional), period days, symptoms, moods, energy, notes, habits, planned events, aura check-ins and settings. Period and symptom logs are health data, and we treat them with special care.

Without an account

All of this is saved only in the app on your device. We never receive it. If you delete the app, clear its data or lose your phone, the data is gone and we cannot restore it. You can export a copy in Profile → “Export my data”.

With an account

When you create an account (with Apple, Google or email and password) and tick the consent box, the app saves a copy of your data to our database so it syncs between your devices.

  • Where: Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union.
  • Who can read it: only you. Each person’s data is locked to their login by database rules. It travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by our provider.
  • What we receive from Apple or Google: your email address and a sign-in ID, nothing else. With Apple you can hide your email.
  • Legal basis (EU/UK): your explicit consent to process health data (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), which you give with the consent box. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
  • How long: until you delete your account. “Delete account” in the app erases your login and all cloud data immediately. The copy on your phone stays until you delete it too.

We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and we do not share data with data brokers, employers or insurers.

Partner sharing

If you choose to share your cycle with a partner or friend, the link contains your first name, cycle estimates (last period start, average cycle and period length, optionally fertile days) and a private code so love signals reach you. If you added them, it also holds your profile photo and the day and month of your birthday, for your star sign. Symptoms, moods and notes are never included. Anyone with the link can view it, so share it only with people you trust.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data in the app at any time. You may also ask us by email for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion. If you live in the EU or UK, you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Underskin is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Not medical advice

Predictions and tips are estimates based on your logs and traditional knowledge. Underskin is not a contraceptive and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
