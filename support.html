Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays only on your phone. With a free account your data is saved and comes back on any new phone.

How do I move to a new phone?

Create an account on your old phone (Profile → Account), then sign in on the new one. Without an account, use Profile → “Export my data” first.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Account → “Delete account” erases your account and cloud data. Profile → “Delete all data” erases everything on the phone.

Why is my prediction off?

Predictions learn from your logged periods. Log a few cycles and they get more accurate. Under the Skin is not a contraceptive.
