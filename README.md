# Under the Skin

A women's hormone & cycle app — live in sync with your hormones, with cycle tracking, phase-based nourishment, Traditional Chinese Medicine rituals and seasonal foods.

**Open it:** https://elijanaru.github.io/under-the-skin/ — then *Share → Add to Home Screen* (iPhone) or *Install app* (Android).

Private by design: no account, no server, no tracking. Everything you log stays on your own phone.

Recipe photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com). Not medical advice and not a contraceptive.
